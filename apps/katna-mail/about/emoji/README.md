# Emoji in About

The faces and the sparkles in the little play inside "Buy me a coffee"
(`src/window/about/coffee.rs`): U+1F622 crying face, U+1F60A smiling face
with smiling eyes and U+2728 sparkles, 64 px, from
[Noto Emoji](https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji) by Google, under the
Apache License 2.0, as packaged in `emoji-datasource-google` 16.0.0.

They are pictures rather than text so they show in colour on every desktop:
GPUI draws an emoji from the first font that has it, and a black-and-white
font such as DejaVu Sans often comes first.
